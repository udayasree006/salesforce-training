## What is CRM

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

CRM helps companies:
- Manage customer data
- Track sales
- Improve customer communication
- Store business information

---

## Why Companies Use Salesforce

Companies use Salesforce to:
- Manage customer relationships
- Improve productivity
- Track business workflow
- Store customer records safely

---

## Salesforce Concepts

### Account
Represents a company or organization.

### Contact
Represents a person related to an account.

### Opportunity
Represents a possible sale or business deal.

---

## Business Workflow

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

A lead is a new customer enquiry. After communication, it becomes a contact. If there is a chance of business, it becomes an opportunity. After successful sale, the person becomes a customer.

---

## Real World Mapping - College Admission

- Account = College
- Contact = Student
- Lead = Student enquiry
- Opportunity = Admission chance
